About

I've spent seven years on the front line of customer service and process execution, supporting U.S. and international clients across chat, email, and phone. Along the way, I've learned that good support isn't just about being pleasant on a call — it's about noticing where a process wastes time, and fixing it.

That instinct comes from my training as an industrial engineer. I hold a B.S. in Industrial Engineering from Quezon City University, which gave me a structured way to look at workflows: where the bottlenecks are, how to measure them, and how to redesign them so both the customer and the team win.

Today I bring both sides together — the empathy and communication skill of a career CSR, and the analytical habits of an engineer — to help teams handle more, faster, without losing the human touch.
